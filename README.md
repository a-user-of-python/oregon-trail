# Oregon Trail

A faithful recreation of the classic 1985 trail-to-Oregon journey, rebuilt from
scratch as a native iPad/iPhone app in SwiftUI. All code and text are original;
the gameplay structure, historical landmarks, and events follow the beloved
original design.

## What's in the game

- Choose your profession — Banker ($1600, score ×1), Carpenter ($800, ×2),
  or Farmer ($400, ×3) — name your wagon leader and four companions, and pick
  your departure month (March–July 1847).
- Outfit at Matt's General Store: oxen, food, clothing, ammunition, and spare
  wagon wheels, axles, and tongues.
- Travel day by day across ~2,040 miles: Kansas River, Big Blue River,
  Fort Kearny, Chimney Rock, Fort Laramie, Independence Rock, South Pass,
  Green River, Soda Springs, Fort Hall, Snake River, Fort Boise, the Blue
  Mountains, The Dalles, and the Willamette Valley.
- Set pace (steady / strenuous / grueling) and rations (filling / meager /
  bare bones); manage health, food, and weather.
- Cross rivers by fording, caulking and floating, ferry, or waiting —
  depth rises with rain and disasters can cost supplies, oxen, or lives.
- Random trail events: breakdowns, sickness (dysentery, cholera, typhoid…),
  storms, thieves, wild fruit, helpful strangers, and lonely graves.
- Hunt for meat in a tap-to-shoot mini-game (rabbit, deer, bear, buffalo) —
  carry back at most 200 lbs.
- Buy at forts with trail-markup prices, rest, and accept random trades.
- Deaths pause for a tombstone ("Here lies …"); leader death or a wiped-out
  party ends the journey. Reach Oregon for the classic end-of-trail score.

## Accessibility

Large type throughout, high contrast, and big tap targets by design.

## Building

The Xcode project is generated by `gen_project.py`. CI (GitHub Actions,
standard macOS runner) builds an unsigned IPA as a workflow artifact.
